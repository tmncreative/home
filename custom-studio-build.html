Where It Stops

What the Custom Studio Build does not include.

The price is honest because the scope is. These 5 things are outside a Custom Studio Build. TMN says so on the first call instead of pricing them in and staffing up around them.

01

A brand identity program

Naming, logo design, and a full visual identity system are not part of the tier. TMN builds the site from the brand the firm has, or from the work of a brand designer the firm hires. If the existing mark is weak, TMN will say so and can suggest what a designer should deliver before the site starts.

02

Photography and video production

The build uses the firm's photography or licensed imagery chosen with the firm. A shoot with a photographer or a video crew is arranged and paid separately. TMN will brief the photographer on what the site needs.

03

Dozens of pages on a compressed timeline

A 2-person studio builds a small number of sites at a time. A 40-page site needed in 3 weeks is a job for a larger team, and TMN will decline it rather than promise it. A 6 to 12 page site done properly is the tier's home ground.

04

Paid media and ongoing marketing

Google Ads, social advertising, and email campaigns are not included. After launch, the optional add-ons are narrow and published: site care from $50 per month, the Managed Publishing Portal at $150 per month, and AI Search Optimization at $350 per month, each month to month.

05

Compliance approval

TMN prepares disclosure and disclaimer language for review and places the reviewer's returns exactly as written. TMN does not approve, certify, or file anything, and nothing on this site is legal advice. The firm's compliance consultant or counsel keeps that role.

FAQ

What buyers ask about the top tier.

01

What does a Custom Studio Build cost?

A Custom Studio Build starts at $5,000. Every scope, including a site with dozens of pages or several integrations, is quoted in writing before anything is signed. The separate Full Site Rebuild starts at $3,750, and the Starter Refresh is $2,250 for a homepage-led refresh.

02

How does TMN keep the engagement focused at this price?

The engagement is deliberately scoped around the website and handled by the 2 founders. The price covers the agreed strategy, architecture, copy, design, development, integrations, review rounds, and launch. It does not include a brand identity program, naming, photography or video production, paid media, or an ongoing strategy retainer. The exact deliverables and exclusions are written into the proposal before signing.

03

Is the engagement deep enough for a registered investment adviser or a multi-partner law firm?

For the website, yes. A Custom Studio Build includes discovery on positioning and client segments, page architecture, full copy prepared for the firm's review, disclosure and disclaimer paths for regulated firms, hand-coded design, integrations for scheduling and lead capture, structured data, and launch. For an RIA, the compliance reviewer's returns go into the build word for word, as documented on the Hilpan Moxie Wealth Management case study. Where TMN stops is a full rebrand: if the firm needs a new name and identity system, TMN builds the site and works from the brand a designer or the firm supplies.

04

How long does a Custom Studio Build take?

A Custom Studio Build first review typically targets 7 to 10 days after kickoff, and a Full Site Rebuild first draft typically targets 72 hours. Launch timing depends on content, integrations, compliance review, and approvals, and is confirmed in the project agreement. Revisions go straight to the founder who built the page and are usually turned the same day or the next. For regulated firms, the compliance review cycle is the usual long pole, so the disclosure paths are prepared early rather than at the end.

05

Who owns the website when it is finished?

After full payment, the client receives the website files and project assets TMN created specifically for the project, subject to the signed agreement. Third-party software, services, fonts, stock assets, and other licensed materials remain under their providers' terms. There is no proprietary CMS the firm has to keep paying to keep the site live. Optional site care starts at $50 per month, month to month, and a Managed Publishing Portal for firms that post updates is $150 per month. Neither is required to keep the site.

06

Does a firm's AUM or headcount change whether the Custom Studio Build fits?

Not by itself. Website scope is measured in pages, copy, content migration, integrations, stakeholder review, and any brand identity work. Larger firms often bring more of each, but AUM and headcount rarely change the website scope on their own. An established RIA with a usable name and identity fits when the assignment is the website. A firm that also needs naming, a full visual identity system, original photography and video, or an enterprise platform with multiple properties needs a larger team, and TMN identifies that on the first call rather than after a proposal.

07

Can we see a comparable build before we commit?

Yes. The Hilpan Moxie Wealth Management case study documents an SEC-registered RIA build from first inquiry to launch, with the compliance review path and the client's dated comments. The Ramsay Law Firm case study includes a step-by-step verification chain a buyer can run on the live site. Both clients credit TMN in their own footers, and the verification center lists every credited site. If the firm wants to see the direction before signing, TMN produces a free homepage preview in 48 hours.
