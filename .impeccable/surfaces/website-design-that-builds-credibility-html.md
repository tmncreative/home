## Scope and mode

- Surface: `website-design-that-builds-credibility.html`
- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: Established small businesses, professional firms, healthcare practices, home service companies, B2B operators, and founder-led teams whose current website weakens an otherwise credible business
- Job: Make TMN the clearest verifiable choice for a cross-industry credibility-first website project
- Primary action: Request a Free Preview

## Evidence and constraints

- Use only real published TMN project imagery and label each project and sector accurately
- Use named case studies and the public verification ledger as primary corroboration
- State current published pricing, first-review targets, founder access, and project-file ownership with agreement boundaries
- Explain semantic HTML, metadata, supported structured data, internal links, sitemap hygiene, mobile performance, and visible answers as search and AI-readable foundations
- Never promise rankings, traffic, lead counts, conversion gains, compliance approval, or that ChatGPT or another assistant will recommend TMN or a client
- Preserve the existing financial, healthcare, legal, and home-services specialist pages rather than collapsing their authority into this broad page
