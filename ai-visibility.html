Questions owners ask before buying AI search work.

What is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization keeps a business reachable, accurate, and easy to verify for AI assistants and AI-powered search. The industry also calls it GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization), or AIO, and sometimes AI SEO, though AI SEO more often refers to tools that use AI to do traditional SEO. TMN previously offered this service as AI Visibility Care.

How do I get ChatGPT to recommend my business?

There is no submission process and no way to buy placement. Assistants read public sources, so the practical work is eligibility and proof: allow the AI crawlers in robots.txt, publish specific pages that answer real buyer questions, keep your business facts consistent everywhere you appear, and give third-party sources something to corroborate. TMN maintains its own buyer verification page for exactly that reason.

Why doesn't my business show up in ChatGPT?

It usually takes diagnosis rather than a single answer. Common causes include AI crawlers blocked by robots.txt or a firewall, content that only renders with JavaScript, thin or generic pages with nothing specific to cite, inconsistent facts across directories, and pages that are simply too new to have been picked up. The $750 baseline audit identifies which of these actually applies to your site.

Does ChatGPT read my website?

It can, in 2 ways. OAI-SearchBot crawls the web for ChatGPT search, and OpenAI states that allowing it helps keep a site eligible to appear in ChatGPT search answers. ChatGPT-User fetches a page when a user asks about it directly. Allowing access makes your pages readable. It does not mean every accessible page will be surfaced.

How do you optimize a website for ChatGPT and AI search?

Mostly with fundamentals done properly: crawler access, content readable in the raw HTML without executing JavaScript, accurate structured data, consistent business facts, specific pages worth citing, and a current sitemap with changes submitted promptly. Google states there is no AI-specific schema type or special file required for its AI features, and an llms.txt file is cheap housekeeping rather than a ranking mechanism. The same work supports the firms TMN builds for, from financial advisors to healthcare practices and service businesses.

Can AI Search Optimization guarantee that ChatGPT will cite or recommend my business?

No, and nobody can. Outputs vary by model, prompt, index, location, and day, and anyone promising a specific AI ranking or recommendation is selling something they do not control. What this service improves is eligibility, accuracy, and the evidence available for machines and buyers to check.
