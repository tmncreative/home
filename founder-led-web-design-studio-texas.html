What It Changes

What founder-led actually changes for the firm hiring the studio.

The point is not that small is virtuous. It is that 4 specific things go differently when the person who scoped the project is the person building it.

01

The estimate and the build come from the same head

At an agency the salesperson estimates, the project manager re-scopes, and the designer discovers what was promised. Here the person writing the proposal is the person who will code every page in it, so the scope is what gets built and the price is the price.

02

Feedback goes to the builder, not through a queue

A revision request is read by the person who can make the change, usually the same day. That is how a first draft turns around in days rather than weeks, and why the published timelines above are measured in hours and days.

03

A small number of builds at a time

2 people cannot run 30 projects, so TMN does not. The studio inquiry form asks for target timing because active builds are limited each quarter and a start date is a real commitment, not a slot in a production pipeline.

04

Accountability has a name on it

Trevor's name is on the case studies, both founders are named in the code of this site, and the client sites credit TMN by name in their footers. When something is wrong, there is 1 person to call and 1 person responsible for fixing it.

Ask Every Studio

5 questions that separate founder-led from small-agency, answered on the record.

Ask these on the first call with any studio, including this one. A studio that operates like an agency will answer at least 1 of them vaguely.

01

Will the person on this call personally design and code the site?

Yes. Trevor takes the call and builds the site. There is no one to delegate to.

02

Who writes the copy?

Taylor leads copy and strategy with Trevor, working from your intake and your review. For regulated firms the disclosure language comes from your compliance reviewer or counsel and goes in as they approve it. TMN does not approve or file anything on a client's behalf.

03

How many projects are you running right now?

Ask on the call and you will get the actual number. Active builds are limited each quarter, and if the next open start is later than your timing, TMN says so rather than taking the deposit and stretching.

04

What happens if the founder is unavailable?

There are 2 founders, so a client always has a second person who knows the project. The finished site runs on standard static hosting that does not need daily attention, and the files are yours after full payment, so the site is portable to any host and never depends on a studio staying reachable.

05

When does founder-led stop being enough?

When a project needs 40 pages in 3 weeks, a full brand identity program, or paid media management alongside the site. TMN says that in the first conversation and points you elsewhere. Custom Studio Builds from $5,000 cover the deeper strategy, copy, and disclosure paths that professional firms need, and the studio inquiry form takes planned investments from $5,000 to $20,000 and above.

Founder-led does not mean cheaper by default and it does not mean faster on every project. It means the person accountable for the result is the person doing the work, and you can confirm that before you sign.
