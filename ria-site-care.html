When you should not buy this

If your site is static in the real sense, meaning the content genuinely does not change and you have somewhere reliable to host it, care is an expense without a job. TMN hands over the completed project files after full payment with no proprietary CMS lock-in, so a firm that wants to self-host and leave the site alone can do exactly that. Buy care when there is something to maintain and someone who will need it changed.

Built for firms going independent.

Advisors launching an independent RIA are comparing vendors at the point where every vendor is attentive. The terms above are the ones that still apply a year in, when a disclosure has to move against a filing deadline. Firms at that stage should also read the breakaway advisor website guide and the RIA website compliance guide.

FAQ

Common questions about post-launch care.

What advisory firms ask before they commit to a monthly plan.

How fast does TMN Creative respond after launch?

RIA Site Care carries a target response within 1 business day, direct from Trevor Niemann by email rather than through a ticket system or an account manager. Routine approved changes to existing pages are typically deployed within 2 business days of approved copy. These are stated service targets from a 2-person studio rather than an enterprise SLA with penalties attached, and they are written into the care agreement rather than left to be discovered after launch.

What does RIA Site Care cost?

$100 per month, month to month, with no minimum term and no cancellation fee. Every other service is an add-on that requires an active Site Care plan, so the totals are additive. Adding the Managed Publishing Portal at $150 brings the recommended combined operating setup to $250 per month, adding AI Search Optimization at $200 brings it to $300, and adding Compliance Site Care at $250 brings it to $350.

What counts as a routine change?

Routine means changes to existing pages, such as copy edits, image swaps, and updates to information already on the site. New pages, new sections, new functionality, design work, and content production are quoted in advance or billed at $150 per hour with written approval. Nothing out of scope proceeds without approval first.

What is included each month?

Managed hosting, SSL, automated uptime monitoring, deployment history and rollback, monthly verification of contact-form delivery and the Form CRS and disclosure links, routine technical and security maintenance, routine approved changes to existing pages, direct support from Trevor, and a complete archive of the deployed website at launch and on reasonable request.

Am I locked in?

No. Site Care is month to month with no minimum term, no cancellation fee, and no 12-month contract. If Site Care is canceled, TMN Creative assists with migration and delivers the complete current website package within 5 business days of written cancellation, including the current website files, published content library, deployment configuration, and the information needed for cutover.

Does TMN Creative provide compliance approval?

No. TMN prepares work for the firm's review and the firm remains responsible for legal, regulatory, advertising, and compliance review and approval. Firms that also need a dated monthly website archive and a change log a compliance reviewer can read should look at Compliance Site Care, a separate add-on at $250 per month.

Compare the terms, not the adjectives.

Ask any studio you are considering for its response target, its deployment target for routine changes, its hourly rate for out-of-scope work, and what it hands over if you cancel. Ask for those 4 in writing. TMN publishes all 4 on this page: 1 business day, 2 business days, $150 per hour, and the complete website package within 5 business days.
