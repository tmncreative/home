Homeowners screen roofers hard.

Roofing buyers have been trained by storm chasers and unlicensed crews to look for reasons to say no. The site's first job is to remove every one of them.

Licensing

CSLB License #1146573, insured and bonded, appears in the public page copy rather than living in a PDF a homeowner has to request.

Owner-led

The site names founder Garrett Horton and states that inspections are performed by the owner, which is the operating reality of this family-run company.

Coverage

Service pages cover South Bay, Long Beach, Greater Los Angeles, and Orange County, with city-level pages for Carson, Torrance, Manhattan Beach, Redondo Beach, Hermosa Beach, and neighboring cities.

What TMN built into the site.

Every section answers a screening question a homeowner or property manager is already asking, and every path ends at an inspection request.

  • Credentials in the first screenState licensing, APOC certification, and GAF Certified Plus contractor status appear before any sales copy.
  • A documented inspection offerThe free inspection is described as a deliverable: photos and written condition notes, not a doorstep sales visit.
  • Full service architectureFlat systems (TPO, PVC, torch down, coatings) and pitched systems (shingle, tile, metal) each get real pages, alongside repair, maintenance, and emergency leak response.
  • Direct conversion pathsPhone, inspection booking, an instant estimator tool, and a financing inquiry give different buyers different ways to start.
