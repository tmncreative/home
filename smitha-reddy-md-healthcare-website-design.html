What TMN built into the page.

Every section has a specific trust job, and the patient inquiry path remains available without crowding the clinical story.

  • Credentials in the first screenSpecialty, ABIM board certification, IFM training, and 15+ years of clinical experience appear before the first call to action.
  • A detailed physician profileTraining, clinical interests, philosophy, and educational resources are organized into clear, linkable sections.
  • Responsible patient routingCare applications go to Functional Health Institute, and the closing language distinguishes education from medical advice and emergency care.
  • Search and entity clarityCanonical metadata and Physician structured data connect Dr. Reddy, her specialty, and Functional Health Institute in crawlable public markup.
