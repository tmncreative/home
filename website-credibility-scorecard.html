What the score cannot establish.

A website can expose credibility, but it cannot create facts that do not exist or replace independent diligence.

  • Business qualityThe score measures visible website evidence, not the quality of the underlying company, service, advice, or care.
  • Legal or regulatory approvalLicenses, disclosures, privacy duties, and regulated claims still require the appropriate client, counsel, CCO, or licensing review.
  • Performance outcomesThe score does not predict rankings, speed, accessibility, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue without separate testing and evidence.
  • AI recommendationsNo score or technical change can guarantee that ChatGPT, Google, or another system will cite or recommend a business.

Website credibility scorecard questions.

What counts as verifiable website proof?

Verifiable proof is specific evidence a buyer can inspect, such as named work, live project links, accurate case studies, public reviews, credentials, licenses, team identities, locations, process details, ownership terms, and working contact paths.

What is a good website credibility score?

A score of 10 to 12 means the website makes most important credibility signals easy to inspect. Seven to 9 indicates a credible foundation with meaningful gaps. Four to 6 means buyers must fill in too much context, and zero to 3 means most trust still depends on unsupported claims or an offline conversation. These bands are TMN Creative's working diagnostic, not an industry benchmark.

Does TMN Creative save my scorecard answers?

The scorecard stores checkbox state only in your own browser so the score can persist between visits. It does not ask for a name, website address, email, or account, and the answers are not submitted to TMN Creative.

Does a high credibility score guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will recommend a business?

No. A high score means the website exposes more of the public evidence buyers and systems can evaluate. It does not guarantee rankings, citations, traffic, leads, conversions, or recommendations from ChatGPT, Google, or any other system.

Yes. The scorecard is a free self-audit. You can use the gaps to improve the current website internally, brief another provider, or ask TMN Creative for a free homepage preview.
