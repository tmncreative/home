The practical questions come first.

Someone researching a psychiatry practice may be doing it on a hard day. The site is organized to answer 3 practical questions early: who provides care, which plans may be accepted, and how to start.

Providers

7 named clinicians with their stated credentials, including 3 PMHNP-BC prescribers and 4 licensed therapists, so visitors can see the practice's current care team.

Coverage

Applicable PPO plans from Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, and Cigna are stated on the live site, with plan-specific verification handled by the practice.

Access

Online booking with no referral required, phone intake, an in-person Northbrook location, and telehealth across Illinois.

What TMN built into the site.

The structure is designed to reduce friction, while treatment content is prepared for practice and clinical review.

  • Providers in frontA provider directory with names, photos, and stated credentials so visitors can see who provides care.
  • Plain service architectureTherapy, psychiatry and medication management, ADHD testing, and cognitive screening each get a clear home in the patient's own vocabulary.
  • Regulated treatment contentThe live SPRAVATO page includes published Indications and Important Safety Information. TMN prepared the page for practice and clinical review and does not independently approve medical claims.
  • A no-referral intake pathOnline booking and phone intake stated plainly, with insurance answered up front instead of after the form.
