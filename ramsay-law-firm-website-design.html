A brand-new firm has no record to point at.

Kris opened his own practice after years representing injured people in South Florida. A new firm cannot lean on verdict tallies, review counts, or a long client list, and the honest ones will not invent them. The site had to earn trust some other way.

No borrowed proof

No testimonials, no invented reviews, no stock-photo team. Nothing appears on the site that could not be sourced.

Advertising review is part of the build

Florida regulates attorney advertising in detail. TMN prepared factual content and disclosure structure for the firm's review before launch.

Clients search the problem

Injured people search deadlines, insurance questions, and what to do next long before they search for a lawyer by name.

What TMN built into the site.

The answer to a thin record is verifiable substance. The site publishes the rules that govern a client's case, cites each one, and lets the reader check the lawyer too.

  • The Rulebook15 Florida deadlines and rules written in plain language, each carrying a citation. The homepage leads with the 14-day PIP initial-services window under Fla. Stat. 627.736(1)(a) and the current 2-year period that applies to many general negligence claims under Fla. Stat. 95.11, subject to exceptions and accrual rules.
  • Every legal claim sourcedMore than 20 statements of Florida law across the site, each traced to a statute or case in a project fact sheet: comparative negligence under 768.81(6), the transitory-substance knowledge standard under 768.0755, sovereign immunity under 768.28, and strict products liability under West v. Caterpillar Tractor Co.
  • Advertising considerations prepared for reviewThe responsible lawyer and location are visible. The site avoids unsupported outcomes and expertise claims. TMN used the current Florida Bar website checklist as a review input, while the firm retained final legal and advertising approval. The full checklist is in attorney advertising rules for law firm websites.
  • Credentials the reader can checkThe hero states Florida Bar No. 1002563 and links directly to the Bar directory, so a prospective client can confirm good standing without leaving the decision to trust.
  • No fake anythingThe firm had no published phone number at build time, so calls to action are form-first rather than showing an invented number. No attorney photo shipped, because no current headshot existed.
  • Hand-coded, 18 pages, launch-gatedA single flag held the whole site behind noindex until launch. Practice-area pages, the rulebook, and disclosures were all built as real crawlable pages rather than a one-page template.
