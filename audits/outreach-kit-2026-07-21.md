## Best Partner Targets

Prioritize people who already advise businesses before a website purchase:

1. Fractional CMOs, brand strategists, and marketing consultants.
2. Commercial photographers and video teams that do not build websites.
3. Managed IT providers and cybersecurity firms serving professional offices.
4. CPA consultants, practice-management advisors, and compliance consultants.
5. Commercial lenders, business brokers, and M&A advisors working with growing firms.
6. Sign companies, printers, and branding shops that hear about rebrands first.
7. Local chambers, industry associations, and curated vendor directories.

Avoid bulk outreach lists. Start with people who can identify a real trigger: a rebrand, acquisition, new office, partner transition, poor lead quality, or a website the owner is embarrassed to send.

## 30-Day Cadence

- Week 1: Build a list of 20 warm or adjacent partners and send five personal messages.
- Week 2: Send five more and publish one useful buyer-guide post using the Fort Worth or financial-advisor guide.
- Week 3: Follow up once with week-one contacts and ask two existing clients for introductions to one business owner each.
- Week 4: Review replies and tracked visits, keep the best-performing partner category, and stop using any message that attracts low-budget leads.

The goal is five thoughtful touches per week, not hundreds of generic emails.
