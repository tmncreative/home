# TMN Creative AI visibility baseline

Audit date: 2026-07-21
Source: Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, United States database

## Baseline

- AI Visibility score: 34
- Mentions: 2
- Citations: 3
- Cited pages: 2
- Mention distribution: ChatGPT 100%; Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode, and Gemini 0% in the Semrush sample
- Cited pages: `/home-services` in two prompts and `/phoenix` in one prompt

## Confirmed strength

For the sampled prompt "Can you recommend some top web design companies specializing in home services?", ChatGPT placed TMN Creative first and cited `/home-services`. The answer specifically recognized TMN's hand-coded, lead-generation focus; home-service specialization; ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, and Workiz integrations; mobile-first conversion work; and full ownership.

This establishes a useful pattern: a focused page with a direct category answer, concrete capabilities, clear fit, and verifiable proof can earn both a mention and a citation.

## Competitor correction

Semrush initially compared TMN Creative with Jobber, Housecall Pro, and ServiceTitan. Those are field-service software platforms, not web-design competitors, so the original 3,800 topic opportunities were mostly irrelevant.

The working comparison was corrected to contractor-focused web agencies: Arete Quanta, CI Web Group, HVAC Websites, and 1TopAgency. That reduced the topic gap to eight, including these useful adjacent buyer questions:

- HVAC marketing and branding
- Plumbing lead generation
- Multi-location marketing and digital strategy
- Roofing PPC advertising readiness
- Carpet-cleaning web design

## Implementation strategy

1. Preserve `/home-services` as the cluster hub because it already earns citations.
2. Publish direct, citation-ready buyer guides for the adjacent questions TMN can answer credibly.
3. State service boundaries clearly. TMN builds the website, landing pages, measurement, and routing foundation; it should not imply that it manages ad buying when it does not.
4. Add visible proof and transparent best-fit guidance, not keyword-only pages.
5. Connect the cluster through visible internal links, the XML sitemap, and `llms.txt`.
6. Pursue third-party mentions separately; on-site content cannot substitute for independent authority.

## Measurement after launch

Record the launch date, submit new URLs through existing indexing workflows, and compare at 30, 60, and 90 days:

- Number of AI prompts that mention TMN Creative
- Number and diversity of cited TMN pages
- Google Search Console impressions and clicks for each guide
- Referring domains and independent mentions
- Qualified inquiries attributed to organic search, AI assistants, and referral sources
