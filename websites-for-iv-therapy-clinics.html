FAQ

Common questions from IV therapy founders.

Answers to what we hear from mobile IV and wellness clinic founders when they reach out.

How much does an IV therapy website cost?

IV therapy web design at TMN Creative starts at $2,250 for a Starter Refresh, $3,750 for a Full Site Rebuild, and $5,000+ for Custom Studio Builds. The final price, pages, integrations, and third-party features are confirmed in the approved scope before work begins.

Why do you build the service menu around symptoms instead of drip names?

Because self-pay clients search for the problem they want fixed: hangover relief, fatigue, immunity, jet lag, recovery. The menu leads with those needs and keeps the clinical formulation details one level down, so the site matches how people actually look for IV therapy.

How do you handle local SEO for a mobile IV service with no storefront?

A mobile operation needs service-area architecture instead of one address: city and neighborhood pages for the areas you actually serve, each with appropriate schema and real content about coverage and response. Multi-city scopes are built as Custom Studio Builds.

Does the website handle patient intake and HIPAA?

The marketing site does not collect medical information. Booking and intake route to your secure, HIPAA-compliant systems, with supported links or embeds for platforms such as IntakeQ, Jane, Acuity, Vagaro, or Hydreight when included in the approved scope. Your privacy officer and vendors govern the intake side.

Can you write the medical claims on the site?

We prepare factual, restrained copy and layouts for your review, including medical director and nursing credentials presented prominently. Your medical director, legal counsel, and licensing authority interpret applicable rules and give final approval. TMN does not provide medical or compliance approval.

Can you present memberships and packages?

Yes. Recurring wellness memberships, packages, and event or group bookings can each get clear presentation and comparison, with checkout and scheduling handled by the platforms you already use.

Are TMN Creative IV therapy sites prepared for AI-assisted search and ChatGPT?

AI-readable foundations are included where appropriate to the visible content and approved scope. We can use LocalBusiness and Service schema, source-labeled reviews, and visible question-and-answer sections when the page content supports them. These foundations help search and AI systems interpret the site, but they do not guarantee inclusion or recommendation.
