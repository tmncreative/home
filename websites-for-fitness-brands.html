FAQ

Common questions from fitness brand owners.

Answers to what we hear from fitness founders and education companies when they reach out.

How much does a fitness brand website cost?

Fitness brand web design at TMN Creative starts at $2,250 for a Starter Refresh, $3,750 for a Full Site Rebuild, and $5,000+ for Custom Studio Builds. The final price, pages, integrations, and third-party features are confirmed in the approved scope before work begins.

Can you restructure a cluttered fitness site so it is easier to navigate?

Yes. We map the current content, cut and consolidate pages, and rebuild the navigation around the approved programs, products, and audience paths so each has one clear home.

Do you integrate with Mindbody, Zen Planner, PushPress, or Kajabi?

We can connect to platform-provided signup pages, booking links, or supported integration points for systems such as Mindbody, Zen Planner, Glofox, PushPress, Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific, and Uscreen when those connections are available and included in the approved scope. The exact flow depends on vendor capabilities, credentials, and the signed agreement.

Can you build a site for an online course or certification program?

Yes. We build the public marketing site: program pages, curriculum outlines, instructor credibility, comparison tables, and enrollment paths that hand off to the course platform you already use. The course delivery itself stays on your LMS.

Do you build online stores for supplements or equipment?

Large ecommerce catalogs are not what this studio builds. If your brand sells products, the site links cleanly into your existing store on Shopify or a similar platform, and the marketing site does the positioning and education work around it.

Are TMN Creative fitness sites prepared for AI-assisted search and ChatGPT?

AI-readable foundations are included where appropriate to the visible content and approved scope. We can use Service and Organization schema, source-labeled reviews, and visible question-and-answer sections when the page content supports them. These foundations help search and AI systems interpret the site, but they do not guarantee inclusion or recommendation.
