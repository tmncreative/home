# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Established small businesses, professional firms, healthcare practices, RIAs, attorneys, and service operators evaluating a website redesign or a custom operations platform. Prospective clients often arrive through referrals, Google, or AI-assisted search and need to establish credibility before contacting the studio.

## Product Purpose

TMN Creative presents the work, pricing, process, expertise, and proof of a founder-led Fort Worth web design studio. The site should help a serious operator understand the studio quickly, verify relevant experience, and choose a clear next step.

Success means qualified prospective clients can find TMN Creative, understand its fit for their industry, verify real work, and submit a project inquiry or free-preview request with accurate attribution.

## Positioning

TMN Creative is a husband-and-wife studio that combines custom visual direction, hand-coded implementation, clear positioning, SEO and AI-readable foundations, and a fast founder-led workflow. Clients work directly with the people designing, writing, and building the site. After full payment, clients can take ownership of the completed project files and assets created for their project, subject to the signed agreement.

## Operating Context

Prospects compare TMN Creative with freelancers, template platforms, industry-specific vendors, and traditional agencies. Trust depends on visible client work, clear service and pricing information, credible founder context, mobile performance, accessible forms, and corroboration from independent client sites and public profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site deployed through Netlify
- Netlify Forms for project inquiries and free-preview requests
- Fathom and Plausible analytics plus first-party attribution logic
- Search and AI-readable foundations including semantic HTML, metadata, structured data, sitemaps, internal links, and `llms.txt`
- Website projects start at $2,250, with larger rebuild and custom studio tiers published on the pricing page
- Do not invent client outcomes, rankings, conversion gains, revenue, testimonials, credentials, or compliance claims
- Do not publish private client information or expand a client engagement beyond public evidence without permission
- The public founding year is 2025, and the current team is Trevor and Taylor Niemann, a two-person husband-and-wife studio. External profiles that conflict with those facts should be corrected rather than copied into owned content

## Brand Commitments

- Name: TMN Creative
- Legal entity: True Margin North, LLC

## Product Principles

1. Prove expertise with specific, verifiable work before making broad claims.
2. Keep every conversion path clear, measurable, and honest about attribution limits.
3. Make regulated and trust-first industries easy to understand without overstating compliance.
4. Preserve ownership, accessibility, performance, and search foundations in every change.
5. Prefer a small number of evidence-rich pages over thin directory-style expansion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive alternative text, and mobile-first form usability. Target WCAG 2.2 AA where the implementation supports it.
