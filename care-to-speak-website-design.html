A preview built for two audiences.

The design brief covers parents researching therapy for a child and adults seeking help for themselves. The preview gives each audience a clear route without splitting the practice in two.

Credentials

The preview presents supplied M.S., CCC-SLP, California licensure, ASHA certification, and 20+ years of experience content prominently.

Lifespan care

Supplied service content for children, teens, and adults is arranged in one coherent structure.

Reach

The supplied brief describes South Bay in-person therapy and remote care. The preview keeps those paths distinct.

What TMN built into the site.

The intended UX reduces ambiguity for someone considering a first call and gives each major content path a consultation next step. No booking outcome is claimed.

  • Credentials in the first screenCCC-SLP certification, state licensure, and two decades of clinical experience appear before any call to action.
  • Plain-language service pagesClinical services are described in the words families actually use, from articulation and language delays to executive function and swallowing.
  • A how-it-works pathThe process from first consultation to ongoing therapy is laid out step by step, which is what an anxious first-time caller needs most.
  • Direct consultation bookingCalendly scheduling, phone, and email give families and adult clients a first step that does not require a form-and-wait cycle.
