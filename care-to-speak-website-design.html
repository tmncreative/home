Two audiences, one clinician.

Parents researching therapy for a child and adults seeking help for themselves arrive with different questions. The site answers both without splitting the practice in two.

Credentials

M.S., CCC-SLP, California licensure, ASHA certification, and 20+ years of experience are stated plainly, because families check credentials before anything else.

Lifespan care

Articulation, language delays, social communication, executive function, cognitive-communication, and swallowing services are explained for children, teens, and adults in one coherent structure.

Reach

In-person therapy serves the South Bay while remote sessions extend the practice nationwide, and the site presents both without confusing either audience.

What TMN built into the site.

Every section lowers the barrier for someone who has been putting off this call, and every path ends at a consultation booking.

  • Credentials in the first screenCCC-SLP certification, state licensure, and two decades of clinical experience appear before any call to action.
  • Plain-language service pagesClinical services are described in the words families actually use, from articulation and language delays to executive function and swallowing.
  • A how-it-works pathThe process from first consultation to ongoing therapy is laid out step by step, which is what an anxious first-time caller needs most.
  • Direct consultation bookingCalendly scheduling, phone, and email give families and adult clients a first step that does not require a form-and-wait cycle.

This case study stays inside the evidence. It does not claim caseload growth, clinical outcomes, or private project metrics.
